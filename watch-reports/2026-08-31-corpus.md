## Scope note

The watch compares the full `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header value byte-for-byte. Response-envelope headers stored alongside it in the fixtures (date, request ids) are snapshot metadata and are not compared. Synthetic fixtures (constructed bazaar cases, `example.test` hosts) are excluded automatically — they exercise the parser and make no claim about any live endpoint.
